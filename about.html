About QR Code Generator & Scanner

Generate QR Scan QR Code

QR Pretty is a free, open-source tool that allows you to create and scan QR codes quickly and easily. With a wide range of styling options and no limitations, you can create and scan QR codes for various purposes without any paywalls or registration requirements.

Features

  • QR Code Generator - Create customizable QR codes with various styles, colors, and features
  • QR Code Scanner - Quickly scan QR codes using your camera or from uploaded/pasted images
  • Logo integration within QR codes
  • Multiple download formats (PNG, JPEG, SVG, PDF, TIFF)
  • Bulk QR code generation
  • Adjustable error correction levels
  • Completely free with no limitations

Why Choose QR Pretty?

Unlike other QR code services, QR Pretty offers both generation and scanning capabilities completely free. We don't limit functionality behind paywalls or require user registration. Our tool is designed to be user-friendly while providing professional-grade features for both generating and scanning QR codes.
